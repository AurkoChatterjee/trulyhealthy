About this project

TrulyHealthy is a full-stack Electronic Health Record (EHR) system: patients book, cancel and reschedule appointments; doctors manage patient history and treatment; administrators manage accounts; and research/government officials get anonymized, aggregate health statistics.

Tech stack

Known limitations

Built as a portfolio project, with a few deliberate simplifications worth naming up front: passwords are hashed with salted SHA-256 rather than a dedicated algorithm like bcrypt/argon2; sessions are plain server-side tokens with no expiry; appointment slot conflicts are checked on exact timestamp match rather than duration-aware overlap; there's no email/SMS notification layer; and the research dashboard exposes aggregate counts only — no per-patient data — by design.
